This is high-risk construction work — you need a SWMS before starting. No SWMS yet? Make one in 2 minutes.
Asbestos: don't drill, cut, sand or break suspected material. Work around it or stop and get it tested. Removal only by a licensed removalist (small amounts of non-friable asbestos have strict rules — check with your regulator).
Gas: licensed gasfitter only. Isolate and make safe before you start, no ignition sources, leak-test before you leave.
Trench over 1.5 m: sides must be supported, benched or battered before anyone goes in. In Victoria, WorkSafe must be notified at least 3 days before this work starts.
Confined space: don't go in unless you're trained, the space has been assessed and you have an entry permit and a standby person. Never go in to rescue someone — call 000.
Structural work: temporary props must be in place before anything load-bearing is cut or removed. Get an engineer's or builder's direction if you're not sure what's holding what up.
Silica dust: use on-tool water or dust extraction and a fitted P2 mask. No dry sweeping. Engineered stone benchtops: installing new ones is banned; cutting existing ones needs strict controls.

About this tool

Terms of use · Privacy

Privacy

SafeTradie doesn't collect your information or use it for anything. What you enter stays on your device. Your name, contact details, licence numbers and state are saved in this browser so you don't have to retype them. Job answers, signatures and photos aren't saved once you start a new job. When you tap Send, the PDF and summary are emailed to the addresses you enter through our email delivery service (Resend). We don't keep a copy or use them for anything else; the delivery service keeps short-term sending records. Our web host (Netlify) keeps standard visit logs, such as IP addresses, like any website.
